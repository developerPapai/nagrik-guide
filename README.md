# Nagrik Guide

**Nagrik Guide** is an offline-first app that offers general legal information for people in India. It provides English and Hindi guides designed to help users understand their rights and prepare for common interactions.

> **Important:** This app provides general information, not legal advice. Laws and procedures can change. The app’s content has not been reviewed by a lawyer; verify important information with a qualified lawyer or an official source.

## Download for Android

[**Download the latest Android APK**](https://github.com/developerPapai/nagrik-guide/actions/runs/37972574946/artifacts/11636807281)

To install:

1. Download the `nagrik-guide-apk` artifact. GitHub downloads it as a ZIP archive.
2. Extract the ZIP file and locate the `.apk` file.
3. Open the APK on your Android device and follow the installation prompts. Android may ask you to allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.

If the download link has expired, open [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/developerPapai/nagrik-guide/actions), select the latest successful **Build Android APK** run, and download its `nagrik-guide-apk` artifact. GitHub artifacts are temporary and may expire.

## Features

- Works offline after installation.
- English and Hindi interface and content.
- Practical guides, rights information, and prepared scripts.
- Article finder and reference pages.
- Personal notes, contacts, and checklists saved on the device.
- Readability controls and high-contrast display options.

## Privacy

The app is designed to work offline and does not require an account. Personal notes, contacts, and checklist data are stored on your device. Review the app’s privacy and security information before entering sensitive details.
